The Calling Conventions

Unlike Linux and BSD, 9front does not follow the System V ABI, so there are some differences in how the arguments are passed to the functions.

For sycalls:

syscall number: RBP
arg0: 0x8+RSP
arg1: 0x10+RSP
...

For libc functions:

arg0: RBP
arg1: 0x8+RSP
...
